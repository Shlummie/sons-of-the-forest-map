# Source and provenance register

The committed baseline uses only synthetic markers and original artwork. This register documents research leads used by the optional importer and prior design investigation; it does not claim that every linked source is bundled or licensed for redistribution.

| Source | Purpose | Public baseline use | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Synthetic demonstration data | Exercise schema, filters, details, search, and progress | Three fictional records are committed | Not gameplay data |
| [lmachens/sons-of-the-forest-map](https://github.com/lmachens/sons-of-the-forest-map) | Optional structured-data importer and coordinate normalization reference | No upstream records committed | Upstream currently identifies the project as MIT; still review current license, field provenance, linked assets, and accuracy before distribution |
| [Official Steam news](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1326470/allnews/) | Version chronology | Link/reference only | Primary announcements, but individual pages and dates change |
| [Sons of the Forest Wiki: Updates](https://sonsoftheforest.wiki.gg/wiki/Updates) | Patch cross-check | Link/reference only | Community-maintained |
| [Sons of the Forest Wiki: Locations](https://sonsoftheforest.wiki.gg/wiki/Locations) | Terminology and relationship leads | Link/reference only | Community content can lag patches and has its own license |
| [Sons of the Forest Wiki: Map](https://sonsoftheforest.fandom.com/wiki/Map) | Historical basemap provenance investigation | No map asset committed | A page license does not automatically license an underlying game image |
| MapGenie and editorial guides | Coverage/terminology cross-checks | No records, prose, screenshots, or assets copied | Proprietary/community material; do not bulk copy |

Access dates in `src/data/sources.ts` describe the earlier research snapshot. Refresh and verify those sources before making current gameplay claims.
