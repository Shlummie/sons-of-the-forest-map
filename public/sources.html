How to read the data

Coordinates are normalized from the source project’s world range of -2000..2000 into 0..1. Cave and bunker coordinates stay in separate interior contexts and are never silently projected onto the surface map. Confidence, coordinate precision, spawn type, and unresolved review status are explicit fields.

The repository includes the full audit trail in SOURCES.md, DATA_METHODOLOGY.md, UNRESOLVED_LOCATIONS.md, and VERIFICATION_REPORT.md.
